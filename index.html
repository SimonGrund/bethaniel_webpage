Run a job

Choose one of the four below.

  • Find errors

    Spelling, grammar, punctuation and the little words that fell out. Each fix shown for you to accept or dismiss.

  • Improve my writing

    A report on your habits as a writer: rhythm, and showing against telling. Advice, not corrections — nothing in your text changes.

  • Final readthrough

    A last surface pass before publishing: the typos a reader would notice, and chapters duplicated, missing or cut short.

  • Translate

    Your manuscript in another language, on a much larger model. In the cloud only — the local model is not good enough at it.

About the team

Hi, I am the team!

My name is Simon — I have a Ph.D. in Data Science, and a bunch of experience in software development by now. But let's skip the boring stuff…

I am also the author of the Worldsea Trilogy which you can (and totally should) go buy right now. I love writing stories but I hate fixing typos. That's why I created Betty — so that more stories can come to life, even the ones that do not have the financial backing to hire professional editors.
