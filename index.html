Why Betty

A private copy editor on your machine

I built Betty to do the job I wanted from a copy editor.

Free and private

Betty runs fully on your own machine — no login, no internet, no account, no subscription. Free for personal use and small businesses. Or hand a single job to a cloud in France, which keeps no copy of your writing, temporary or otherwise. Either way you keep every right to your manuscript, and nothing you write trains anything.

The editor you wished you had

For the pass before you send to a publishing house, or before you self-publish. Betty reads prose the way an editor does: she catches the usual grammar gremlins, and she notices when a sentence is doing too much work, or not enough.

Measured, not claimed

Betty surfaces 91% of mistakes, excluding commas and gets the right fix for 93% of them. The benchmark is public, including what she does badly. See the numbers →

You stay in the loop

Betty suggests — she never overwrites. Every correction waits for your yes, and the ones she is unsure of arrive marked as unsure.

About one kettle.

Editing a 60,000-word novel uses roughly the electricity it takes to boil a kettle.* The bigger saving is the hours: the same pass by hand is twenty to forty at a desk. You still read every suggestion — but in about one. * Estimated, not measured: about 643,000 tokens for a 60,000-word copy edit, at published energy-per-token figures. Translation is the exception: it runs on a far larger model in a data centre, and is not one kettle.

Getting started

Four steps. Then just write.

1

Download

Grab the installer for your system above. It's one file — no package manager, no dependencies to chase down.

2

Install

Open the installer and follow along. First thing you have to do, once Betty is installed, is select the size of Betty you think your machine can handle. Don't worry, you can always change your mind later.

3

Open your writing

Add your manuscript and a description of the style you're aiming for. Betty works with plain text, Markdown, and most common document formats. With the style document, you can give custom instructions such as "Don't correct the pirates. They are supposed to talk like that."

4

Let her read

Hit Start Job and Betty will go through your work. Suggestions come up for you to evaluate — accept them, ignore them, your choice.

A few notes

What Betty is — and isn't.

I think Betty is good. I also think it's important to be clear about where she helps, where she doesn't, and what you're signing up for.

Read the full disclosure

Betty is a tool, not a professional. She is a writing aid — not a qualified copy editor, proofreader, or legal reviewer. Her suggestions are starting points, not verdicts. The final call on every sentence is yours.

She will sometimes be wrong. Language is context-dependent in ways that are genuinely hard. Betty will occasionally flag something that was fine, miss something that wasn’t, or suggest a change that makes things worse. Treat her output as a second opinion, not a final edit.

Local by default; the cloud only if you ask. Left to herself, Betty processes text entirely on your own hardware and nothing is transmitted. The exception is a cloud job — opt-in per job, and paid for before it runs: the passage being edited goes, encrypted, to European infrastructure that writes it to no database and no log. Either way you keep every right to your manuscript and to the edited result, and nothing you write is used to train any model. The cloud terms say plainly what happens to the text.

Running locally requires capable hardware. The local model needs at least 8 GB of RAM and approximately 3 GB of free disk space. On older or lower-spec machines she may run slowly or not at all. She does real computational work — there is no lightweight version. The cloud option is for exactly those machines: the model runs in the data centre, and nothing but the app itself has to fit on yours.

Supported platforms. Betty is currently available for macOS 12 or later, Windows 10 (64-bit) or later, and Linux systems running glibc 2.31 or newer. Other platforms are not supported at this time.

Betty is free, and intends to stay that way. Running Betty on your own machine has no usage limit, no subscription and no trial period, and that is the product. There is one paid option — a cloud service, charged per job, for machines that cannot run a model locally — and it is never required to use Betty. If anything about the free version changes, it will be announced here before it takes effect, with enough notice to make a considered decision.

No warranties. Betty is provided as-is. I make no guarantees about accuracy, fitness for any particular purpose, or uninterrupted availability. Use her at your own discretion.
